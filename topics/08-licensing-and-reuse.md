# Licensing and Reuse

Explore what builders need to understand about using other people’s code and sharing what they create.

## Risks to consider

- Code or packages described as free may still carry conditions on use or sharing.

- AI may give incorrect information about a license or the source of a code snippet.

- Required notices or attribution may be lost when components are copied or combined.

- Images, fonts, templates, and datasets may have separate reuse terms.

- Sharing code publicly may happen without checking agency authority or rights.

- People may assume that visible source code automatically comes with permission to reuse it.
