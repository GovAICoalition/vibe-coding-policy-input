# Branding Standards

*Suggested section for discussion*

Consider how a vibe coded tool should reflect the organization’s identity and help users understand who provides it and whether it is ready for use.

## Risks to consider

- AI generated designs may use incorrect logos, colors, fonts, or organization names.

- Inconsistent branding may confuse users or make a legitimate tool appear untrustworthy.

- Official branding may make an early prototype look like an approved organizational service.

- Copied or generated images may introduce another organization’s branding or assets without permission.

- Visual choices may conflict with accessibility needs, such as readable contrast and text size.

- Unclear naming or missing contact information may leave users unsure who provides or supports the tool.
