# Privacy and Data Handling

Consider what information is used while building the tool and what happens to information once people start using it.

## Risks to consider

- Real resident, employee, or agency information may be pasted into an AI prompt while troubleshooting.

- The tool may collect more information than it needs.

- Private details may appear in logs, error messages, downloads, or shared screens.

- AI tools, hosting providers, or plugins may receive or retain information in unexpected ways.

- Information may remain in test copies or backups after it is no longer needed.

- A member example may reveal an agency, person, or sensitive situation even when names are removed.
