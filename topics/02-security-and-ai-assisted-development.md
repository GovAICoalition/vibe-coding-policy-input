# Security and AI Assisted Development

Explore the basic security practices that help protect a tool, its users, and the systems it connects to.

## Risks to consider

- Passwords or access keys may end up in prompts, code, or shared files.

- Generated code may allow someone to view data or take actions they should not be able to.

- Suggested packages may be unsafe, outdated, or different from what the AI describes.

- A coding assistant with broad permissions may change files, install software, or publish something unintentionally.

- Instructions hidden in files or websites may steer a coding assistant into exposing information or taking unrelated actions.

- If AI also runs inside the finished tool, misleading inputs may cause data leaks or unauthorized actions.
