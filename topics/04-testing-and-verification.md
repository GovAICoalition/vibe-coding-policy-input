# Testing and Verification

Explore how someone can build confidence that a tool works beyond the first successful demonstration.

## Risks to consider

- A tool may work for one example but fail with blanks, unusual inputs, or larger amounts of data.

- AI generated tests may repeat the same mistaken assumptions as the generated code.

- A convincing result may hide an incorrect calculation or misunderstood business rule.

- A small change may break something that worked earlier.

- A failed connection or repeated click may lose information or create duplicate actions.

- If AI runs inside the tool, answers or actions may vary even when the task looks the same.
