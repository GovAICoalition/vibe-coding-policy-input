# Accessibility

Consider how vibe coded tools can be usable by people with disabilities, including the complete task and any files the tool creates.

## Risks to consider

- A tool may look fine but be difficult or impossible to use with a keyboard or screen reader.

- Missing labels, unclear errors, poor contrast, or confusing focus order may block a task.

- Text enlargement or a smaller screen may hide controls or content.

- An automated checker may miss barriers that appear during actual use.

- A form may be usable while its dialogs, downloads, or generated documents are inaccessible.

- AI generated interfaces may not meet the accessibility requirements that apply to the agency.
