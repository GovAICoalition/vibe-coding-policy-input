# Documentation and Traceability

Explore the small amount of useful information someone else would need to understand, use, or continue working on the tool.

## Risks to consider

- Only the creator may know what the tool does or how to run it.

- Important setup steps or limitations may exist only in an AI conversation.

- AI written explanations may not match what the code actually does.

- People may use a tool beyond its intended purpose because its limits are unclear.

- The shared version may differ from the version that was tested.

- Useful context may be lost when the creator moves on or access to the coding service ends.

## Ideas for the guidance

What belongs in a short note that travels with the tool? Think about purpose, setup, limitations, changes, and where someone can get help.
