# Maintenance Ownership and Decommissioning

Consider what keeps a shared tool useful over time and what happens when it is no longer supported or needed.

## Risks to consider

- A tool may remain in use after its creator leaves, with nobody able to fix it.

- Package updates or changes to connected services may break working features.

- Hosting, subscriptions, or AI usage may create unexpected ongoing costs.

- A bad change may be difficult to undo without a saved working version.

- Errors or outages may go unnoticed, or nobody may know how to stop the tool.

- An abandoned tool may keep exposing data, retaining access, or incurring charges.
