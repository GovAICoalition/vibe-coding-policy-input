# Risk Tiering and Release Authority

*Suggested section for discussion*

Consider how the level of care should change based on what a vibe coded tool does and who could be affected.

## Risks to consider

- A tool that looks simple may still expose private data or change important records.

- A successful personal prototype may be shared before anyone considers the broader impact.

- An internal tool may influence decisions about residents, employees, or services.

- New users, data, or connections can make a previously low risk tool more consequential.

- Too little review can miss harm; too much process can discourage useful experimentation.

## Ideas for the guidance

What would distinguish a low risk experiment from a tool that needs more help before sharing? A public text formatter and a tool that changes eligibility records offer useful comparison points.
