# Ethics Bias and Human Oversight

*Suggested section for discussion*

Consider how a tool might affect people and how users can recognize, question, or correct harmful results.

## Risks to consider

- Generated code may contain assumptions that unfairly exclude or disadvantage people.

- Rules or defaults may work for common situations while missing others.

- People may trust a polished tool more than its accuracy or limitations justify.

- A person may be expected to approve a result without enough information to check it.

- People affected by an error may have no clear way to question or correct it.

- Bias can be built into ordinary code even when no AI runs in the finished tool.
